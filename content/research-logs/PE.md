## Findings and judgement calls

- **Entry into force:** the Regulation applies "from 120 calendar days following publication" (30 Nov 2024). Counting gives 30 March 2025; secondary summaries say 31 March 2025. No `effectiveDate` is recorded until counsel confirms.
- **DPO phasing:** the table in the First Final Provision counts from *publication*. That gives 30 Nov 2025 (>2,300 UIT), 30 Nov 2026 (1,700–2,300 UIT), 30 Nov 2027 (150–1,700 UIT) and 30 Nov 2028 (micro). The PDF column layout was scrambled, so the bands were reconstructed from the text. **Counsel should verify.**
- **"Large volumes":** the Regulation gives no number. The rule uses ≥100,000 individuals, or any sensitive data, as a proxy, and says so.
- **Day counting:** Art. 69 says "días". Under Peru's administrative procedure law these are likely business days, but that was not verified. Deadlines are stated as in the text.
- **Adequacy:** I did not check whether Peru's Authority has issued adequacy resolutions (Art. 19) covering the EU or Italy. The transfer item therefore assumes that guarantees are needed.
- **Regulator URL:** a dedicated ANPD page on gob.pe was not confirmed, so the Ministry of Justice page is used.
