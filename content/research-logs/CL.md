# Research log — Chile (CL)

- **Researched:** 2026-09-27 by claude-research. **Status:** pending counsel review. All obligations are `needs_review`.

## ⚠ Headline

- **Law 21.719 enters into force on 1 December 2026**, about 9 weeks after this review.
- On **1 September 2026** the Government announced a bill to postpone this to **1 December 2027** (Ministry of Economy news item). I could not confirm the bill's number or legislative stage: camara.cl returned 403 and the Senate portal did not render. **Treat 1 December 2026 as binding until a postponement law is published.**

## Findings and judgement calls

- **The obligations describe the new regime** (from 1 Dec 2026), each with `deadlineOrTimeline` set. The current Law 19.628 regime is summarised only in `scopeNotes`: it is weak, and it will be superseded before most remediation could finish.
- **Sanctions article pinpoint:** amounts are cited to the BCN synthesis §11. The exact article number in the amended law was not extracted.
- **Regulator URL:** the Agency has no confirmed website yet, so the BCN law page is used as a placeholder.
- **Not encoded:** financial-obligation data (Title III), children (16 quáter), geolocation (16 sexies), and public bodies.
