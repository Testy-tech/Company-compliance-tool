## Findings and judgement calls

- **Exact commencement date:** the notification is *dated* 13 Nov 2025, but the Gazette was digitally signed on 14 Nov and PIB says it was "notified on 14 November". "Eighteen months from the date of publication" therefore gives 13 or 14 May 2027. **2027-05-13 is used as a conservative date. Counsel should confirm.**
- **IN-GOVERNANCE-02 is `unverified`:** the SPDI Rules 2011 PDF on MeitY was unavailable (the page returned HTML). Only the fact that s. 43A continues until s. 44(2) commences is verified.
- **Significant Data Fiduciary** status depends on Government notification. The reference group is unlikely to be designated, so SDF duties are mentioned inside other items rather than as a separate obligation.
- **Penalty amounts** come from PIB. The Schedule of the Act was not text-extracted.
