## Findings and judgement calls

- **Intra-group sharing with HQ** needs two separate analyses: (a) the Art. 17 third-party basis, where the contract ground does not qualify, and (b) the Art. 28-8 cross-border basis, where EU equivalence covers Italy. This is a common gap for GDPR-based groups.
- **CPO qualifications:** Annex 1 exists, but its content (experience years, which controllers are covered) was not read. The obligation defers to counsel.
- **KR-AI-01 is `unverified`:** Art. 37-2 was added in 2023, but its text could not be retrieved.
- **Not encoded:** CCTV rules (Art. 25) — the fetched summary conflicted with my prior understanding, so it was left out until the text is read; security measures (Art. 29); pseudonymised data; and the domestic-agent duty, which does not apply where a Korean subsidiary exists.
