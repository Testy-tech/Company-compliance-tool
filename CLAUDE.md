## Source handling (mandatory)
- Never reproduce text from fetched web pages, statutes, regulator guidance, law-firm publications or templates verbatim or near-verbatim.
- Knowledge-base `requirement`, `actionSteps` and research-log entries must be written in your own words. At most one quote per source, under 15 words, only where the exact wording has legal significance.
- Cite statutes by article/section number + official URL (e.g. EUR-Lex, gesetze-im-internet.de). Do not embed statute text.
- Do not generate LICENSE files or legal boilerplate from memory; leave a TODO for me to add them manually.
- Write large content in small batches (one jurisdiction or one file per step), so a blocked output only loses one step.
